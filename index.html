The shop

What we make

Five to start. Each one takes a photo, a name, your colors, or all three.

  • Mug

    Your photo wrapped right around it. Sublimation print, dishwasher safe.

  • Cap

    Choose the color of every panel, then a name embroidered on the front.

  • Bottle

    Body, lid and band in the colors you pick. Name laser engraved into the coat.

  • Tote bag

    A big flat print area. Lead with a photo or lead with the type.

  • Photo block

    Printed straight onto acrylic, with a short caption engraved on the base.

A gift box that holds two to four of these, with a ribbon and a printed card, comes after.

How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. Pick a thing

    Start from a ready made design or from a blank one. Both take the same time.

  2. Make it yours

    Drop in a photo, type a name, choose your colors. The preview updates as you go, and so does the price.

  3. Approve, then order

    You see the exact image that goes to print and approve it first. Cash on delivery, or pay by transfer.

Delivery
